# Q231 · Does the idle-warm switch get renamed now it also prepares neighbouring drawings — **answered: keep the name**

**Answered 2026-10-09: keep "Prepare playback while idle".**

Raised 2026-10-09 by the UI review of #655, which put the drawings either side
of the playhead under the same switch as the playback warm (the switch exists
for a laptop on battery, and that is idle CPU too). The label is now slightly
narrower than the behaviour.

**Recommendation was the chosen one:** the description under the switch already
says both, and a rename touches the manual's menu path for little clarity. The
alternative was "Prepare frames while idle", with the manual and Configure
changed together.
