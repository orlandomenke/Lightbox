# Q251 · Ink wash and Gouache without the simulation, and thin paint that settles — **answered 2026-10-12**

**Answered: Ink wash is "row 2" and Gouache is "row 7", both as recommended; and the order of work was the assistant's to choose.**

Raised by phase 3 of the paint path, for the last two media still simulated after Watercolor (Q244) and Oil (Q246).

What it blocks: deleting the simulation (phase 4, Q240). While any shipped brush runs it, the worker pass, the raw tip and the lattice all have to stay.

## What was asked

**1. What next?** After the large-brush fix the owner was offered a choice between the assistant's suggestions (the stall at the start of a mixing stroke, then thin paint that settles) and the two media still simulated, and answered: *"Make a decision which direction to go. Either way, you have my approval."* **Decided: the two media**, building the settle effect on the way because ink wash needs it.

**2. Thin paint that settles** - the owner's idea the day before, with a reference: *"a thin ink paint layer that did fill all crevices, as it is so thin the paper texture does show through."* Prototyped then on a sheet the owner saw; built here as `BrushSettings.TextureSettle` (**Settles**, on the Texture option): the mark covers everything and is thinner on the sheet's peaks than in its valleys. The opposite sign to a tooth, and a brush can have both.

**3. Ink wash.** Today's simulated brush above three washes. **Answered: row 2** - Plain paper, settles 0.4, wet edge 0.5, hardness 0.75, flow 0.4. The alternatives were softer and coarser on Fibre, or paler for building tone in layers.

**4. Gouache.** Today's simulated brush above three. **Answered: row 7** - Plain paper, fills at a press of 0.25, settles 0.15, bristles 0.5. The alternatives were the same without bristles (cleaner, flatter), and denser on Fibre with no tone inside the solid areas.

## What it costs

- **No shipped brush runs the simulation any more.** A brush an artist makes can still switch a medium on, and drawings made with one still render with it, until phase 4.
- **Strokes already drawn with the simulated Gouache or Ink wash are untouched for now**; they change when the simulation is deleted, as Q240 accepted.
- **Tests that took a shipped simulated brush as their subject** now build a simulated brush for themselves, and go when the simulation does.
